Building at home

Technology that opens doors in Rwanda

I started Rita Computing & Secretariat with two computers in a village room, to give young people free digital skills. It grew into Marita Foundation, a free platform that brings scholarships, jobs and trainings to every young person in Rwanda, with or without internet.

With Ubuzima Mill Solutions, I am reducing post-harvest losses and creating jobs on the farm, managed with a system I built. And Rwamagana Mall lets families in Rwamagana and across Rwanda shop online and pay with MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or cash on delivery.
