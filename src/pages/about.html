My story

Hi, I'm Martha

I build websites and web apps, I train people on using AI tools, and I make entertaining AI drama movies. I love turning a messy real-world need into something clear and easy to use.

I earned a BSc in Applied Computer Technology (Software Engineering) at USIU-Africa, graduating Magna Cum Laude and on the Dean's List every semester. Along the way I trained at Andela, interned at KCB Bank, worked on GIS projects at GeoIQ Partners and delivered 13+ client projects as an AI Operator at Breedj.

I'm a people person: I enjoy collaboration, meeting new friends and teaching. I tutored Java, Python and C++, and I still mentor students at my former secondary school every year.
